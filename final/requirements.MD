Grading:

The project will be graded on a 10-point scale and will be worth 10% of your final grade. To receive full credit, your project must meet the following criteria:

Use of colors that harmonize and enhance the overall design (1 point)
Good use of layout design and composition (1 point)
Well-executed typography that enhances readability and adds visual interest (2 points)
Real content that is relevant and engaging for the target audience (2 points)
Responsive design that adapts to different screen sizes and devices (1 point)
A polished and professional presentation that is a good reflection of your understanding of web design (1 point)
Clean, well-organized code with proper syntax (1 points)
Published online (1 point)
