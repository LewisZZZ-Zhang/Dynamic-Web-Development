My Interest

I enjoy building Gundam model kits and doing model photography.

Gunpla is a hands-on, step-by-step process that starts from individual plastic parts and gradually becomes a complete figure. I like paying attention to small details during the build, and photographing the finished models helps me explore scale, lighting, and atmosphere.
